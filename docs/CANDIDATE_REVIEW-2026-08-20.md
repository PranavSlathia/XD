# Candidate review — 2026-08-20

This is a point-in-time validation of the new funnel against the real public
five-day `.com` feed. It is not an appraisal, legal clearance, or instruction to
bid. Listings and registration state can change at any time.

## Funnel

| Gate | Count |
|---|---:|
| Raw pending-delete rows | 680,453 |
| Clean alphabetic `.com` names | 342,421 |
| OpenPageRank/referring-domain matches | 207 |
| Automatic `research` verdicts | 3 |
| Acquisition-ready after manual review | **0** |

RDAP independently returned `pending delete` for all three automatic research
hits. Public DropCatch detail returned a live acquisition channel, deadline, and
opening price. Those facts proved purchasability; they did not prove safety or
resale value.

## Automatic research hits — manually rejected

### `classflow.com`

- Automatic evidence: OPR 4.17, 111 referring domains, qualifying `class` +
  `flow` retail-demand floor, live pending-delete channel.
- Manual evidence: ClassFlow is Promethean's named education product. Promethean
  says it sunset the product on 2023-12-31, while current Promethean training and
  school documents still use the name and domain.
- Disposition: **reject — former/current product identity and trademark risk.**
  The apparent authority is inseparable from that product history.

### `financebrokerage.com`

- Automatic evidence: OPR 4.74, 206 referring domains, 10-sale weakest-placement
  demand floor, average-price floor about $1,171, live pending-delete channel.
- Manual evidence: a long-running finance/crypto/forex publication and affiliate
  business used this exact identity. Current third-party company/review profiles
  still describe it as a media platform.
- Disposition: **reject — former operating brand, user/reputation baggage, and
  heightened finance-sector risk.**

### `chilandgroup.com`

- Automatic evidence: OPR 3.14, 167 referring domains, qualifying three-word
  retail-demand floor, live pending-delete channel.
- Manual evidence: the archived root resolved to Chinese adult/spam content.
- Disposition: **reject — explicit spam/adult history.**

## Best remaining observe cases

### `floornut.com` — keep for manual diligence, not a bid recommendation

- OPR 2.86; 82 referring domains.
- NameBio weakest-placement demand floor: 10 retail sales; conservative average
  price floor about $2,678.
- Wayback: content from 2008 through 2025, with hundreds of distinct flooring
  URLs rather than a three-page parking shell.
- Open-web exact-domain searches found little surviving brand residue, which is
  useful but is **not** trademark clearance.
- Missing: independent backlink/anchor export, representative archive-page
  reading, exact/fuzzy trademark and former-owner review, domain-specific comps,
  three real buyers, and a maximum bid.
- Current decision: **observe / first manual research candidate.** The name is
  memorable but semantically odd, so buyer liquidity must be demonstrated.

### `superutils.com` — reject unless rights/history review overturns it

- OPR 2.79; 33 referring domains; long archive history.
- Search results still attribute proprietary Windows utilities and downloadable
  executables to “SuperUtils.com Software,” including ExifCleaner and Audio Speed
  Changer Pro.
- Disposition: **reject/hold — former software brand, copyright/download residue,
  and possible reputation/security expectations.** A generic-sounding name does
  not erase the exact prior business identity.

### `literaryescapism.com` — reject

- OPR 2.62; 54 referring domains; deep 2008–2025 archive.
- The same identity remains attached to an active Goodreads profile, Tumblr,
  author citations, and a 2026 book-blog ranking.
- Disposition: **reject — continuing community/creator identity.**

## Conclusion

The old system's failure was not merely lack of volume. It had no acquisition
inventory, no demand evidence, no source provenance, and no manual rights/history
gate. The corrected pipeline found real expiring domains but correctly produced
**no acquisition-ready name in this cycle**.

The next cycles should continue automatically. The target is two or three names
that survive every gate—not two or three names selected because the operator
wants a quota filled.
